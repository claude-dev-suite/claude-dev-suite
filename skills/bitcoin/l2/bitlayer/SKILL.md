# Bitlayer

Bitcoin L2 with focus on **BTC staking** for yield, plus EVM
smart contracts.

## Architecture

- **ZK rollup** with state commitments to Bitcoin.
- **EVM compatibility** for Solidity contracts.
- **BTC staking** — bridged BTC earns yield via Bitlayer's protocols.
- **Federation peg** (planned BitVM upgrade).

## Position

Among the "BTCFi" Bitcoin L2s competing on yield products, but the
TVL has collapsed. DefiLlama chain TVL peaked at ~$406.9M on
17 December 2024, was ~$361.2M on 1 January 2025, ~$2.98M on
1 January 2026 and ~$463k on 16 September 2026 — a ~99.9% drawdown
from peak. The contraction is sector-wide rather than
Bitlayer-specific (Merlin and BSquared fell on the same curve), but
size any deployment against the current figure, not the 2024
headline numbers.

## Use cases

- **BTC staking** for yield.
- **DeFi protocols** (lending, AMMs).
- **Bridge integrations** to Ethereum L2s.

## Limitations

- **Federation trust** until BitVM bridge.
- **Yield sources** sometimes unclear / require careful audit.
